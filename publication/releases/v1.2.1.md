# Climate Change Knowledge Graph 1.2.1

Released on 2026-11-27: version 1.2.1 of the workflow and of the Climate Change Knowledge Graph (CCKG), archived on
Zenodo at [10.5281/zenodo.24584816](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24584816) (all the releases: [10.5281/zenodo.24305088](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24305088)). Previous release: [1.2.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24561291).

## Changes

### Knowledge graph

- The ontologies are taken at the commit `48d5aaf` of `hacid-project/knowledge-graph` (was `bfb0310`), which changes
  only the metadata of their headers, not their axioms: creators by ORCID (top-level: A. G. Nuzzolese, A. Russo,
  M. Ceriani; data and CCSO: M. Ceriani, A. G. Nuzzolese, in CCSO in place of the IRIs of the old namespace), licence
  CC BY 4.0 in all, version 1.0 of top-level and data (`owl:versionIRI`, `owl:versionInfo`), `owl:versionInfo` 2.1 of
  CCSO, `dcterms:created` and `dcterms:modified`, and the declarations of the Dublin Core terms they use.
- The expected types of the information roles whose values are ranges of literals (a period as two dates, a range of
  numbers or durations; 7 roles, e.g. `ops:ChooseBaselinePeriod/associated-data`) are well-formed OWL class
  expressions: the intersection of their two restrictions is typed `owl:Class`, as the other class expressions, and
  `owl:cardinality` is an `xsd:nonNegativeInteger`, as the RDF mapping of OWL 2 requires (it was an `xsd:integer`).
  Their meaning is unchanged: the class of the datatype restrictions on the datatype of the values, with one list of
  facets (`data-sources/mohc-cvs/mapping/operations.jq`).

### Deployment

- The endpoint loads only the dump `kg.nq.gz` of a release, not `void.ttl` too (`excludeFiles` in
  `deployment/kgserve/config.json`): the description of the KG was in the endpoint twice, also as the graph
  `https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24305088#void.ttl` (2,371 triples for release 1.2.0), a copy of the graph
  `https://w3id.org/climate-change-kg/data/kg` of the dump.

## The knowledge graph

- **Size:** 5,656,515 triples (without inferred ones), in 15 named graphs, plus a named graph with the
  metadata of the KG.
- **Content:** 2,693 simulations (696 global simulations and 1,997 dynamical
  downscalings) that use 91 climate models; 613,551 datasets (6,365 CMIP5, 145,232
  CORDEX-CMIP5, 461,954 UKCP18); 6,854 variables; 169 climate indices from 5
  catalogues; 42 hazard types; 81 operations and 60 methods of climate services.
- **Validation:** checked with the SHACL shapes of `shapes/` (52 node shapes, 107 property
  shapes): 0 violations, 43 warnings, 11 informative results.
- **Archive:** the dump (`kg.nq.gz`, gzipped N-Quads) and its description (`void.ttl`: DCAT, VoID, PROV-O, DQV),
  which says that it is this release, with its DOI.
- **Online:**
  - SPARQL endpoint: <https://w3id.org/climate-change-kg/sparql>
  - resources, which dereference: `https://w3id.org/climate-change-kg/data/...`
  - documentation: <https://w3id.org/climate-change-kg>

## What this tag contains

The tag [v1.2.1](https://github.com/hacid-project/climate-change-kg/tree/v1.2.1) is the commit that built this release: the KG can be rebuilt from it (see the README,
`snakemake --cores 4 build`). The commit is also recorded in the metadata of the KG.
